# Phase 4 focused re-run: cars arrive with a real charging need (ADR-0008)

**Runs:** 50, all finished, none failed (2026-11-22, sweeps `p4-arrival-grid` and `p4-arrival-oracle`, 60–73 min per run).
**Setup:** identical to the Phase 4 main grid ([`reports/p4-main-grid/`](../p4-main-grid/README.md), frozen ADR-0007) except one line: `env.arrival_rule: target` (config `configs/experiments/p4_arrival.yaml`). Every car now arrives at **80 % − the energy the driver takes in the ElaadNL data** (ADR-0008), i.e. at **54.7 %** on average instead of 74.7 %; every car needs charging. Same cars, arrival/departure times and battery sizes as before.

|---|---|---|---|---|

Tested on the same 50 test days in 4 worlds (0 / 5 / 10 / 20 % of drivers leave early without warning, cutting their stay by 10–50 %). Reward as in the main grid (ADR-0006).

## Results

### 1. Score: RiskAware p0.2 − Trusting, same seed (`paired.csv`)
Positive = RiskAware better; p (Holm) corrected over all comparisons in that λ folder. In brackets: the "light charging need" case (main grid, cars arriving at 74.7 %).

| λ | Test world | Difference | 95 % CI | Wins / 10 | p (Holm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 500 | 0 % early | −2.32 (−1.38) | ±1.97 | 1 | 0.077 |
| 500 | 10 % early | +4.65 (+2.44) | ±2.72 | 9 | 0.015 |
| 500 | 20 % early | **+10.26** (+5.52) | ±3.68 | **10** | **<0.001** |
| 1000 | 0 % early | −1.12 (−2.60) | ±4.54 | 1 | 0.59 |
| 1000 | 10 % early | +7.81 | ±3.17 | 9 | 0.001 |
| 1000 | 20 % early | **+16.78** (+8.75) | ±4.17 | **10** | **<0.001** |

- With a real charging need, **the benefit of RiskAware roughly doubles** (λ 500: +5.5 → +10.3; λ 1000: +8.8 → +16.8 at 20 % early), and it is now significant after correction already at 10 % early.
- The "insurance" cost when nobody leaves early stays small (1–2 points, not significant after correction).
- **Gap to the Oracle (λ 500, 20 % early):** Trusting −60.5, RiskAware −50.3, PPO-Oracle −43.1 → RiskAware closes **59 %** of the gap (60 % in the light case). The Oracle is flat across worlds (−42.3 to −43.1).
- **RiskAware vs the perfect planner that trusts announcements** (Optimum-announced, knows all prices in advance, single deterministic run): at 20 % early λ 500 −50.3 vs −49.3 (equal), at λ 1000 **−57.4 vs −71.7** (RiskAware better). Knowing that drivers may leave early is worth more than knowing tomorrow's prices.

### 2. Battery level when cars leave, 20 % early world (`drivers.csv`, `summary.csv`)
Mean over 10 seeds (95 % CI for the share below 70 %). In brackets: light charging need case (Oracle there: p0.1).

| λ | Agent | All cars | Early leavers | Worst 10 % of early leavers | Early leavers below 70 % | Money per day (€) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| any | AFAP-target | 81.0 % | 81.0 % | 80.2 % | 0.6 % | −47.86 |
| 500 | Optimum-real | 78.6 % | 78.7 % | 77.3 % | 0.6 % | −23.55 |
| 500 | Optimum-announced | 79.6 % | 83.2 % | 57.7 % | 21.7 % | −25.54 |
| 500 | PPO-Trusting | 80.0 % | 78.0 % | 61.7 % | 25.7 ± 2.1 % (18.9) | −33.48 |
| 500 | RiskAware p0.2 | 81.1 % | 82.6 % | 69.1 % | **11.8 ± 1.0 %** (9.5) | −35.26 |
| 500 | PPO-Oracle p0.2 | 80.1 % | 80.4 % | 74.0 % | 2.7 ± 0.6 % (1.9) | −34.23 |
| 1000 | PPO-Trusting | 82.6 % | 80.2 % | 63.9 % | 20.6 ± 2.0 % (13.7) | −38.33 |
| 1000 | RiskAware p0.2 | 84.5 % | 86.0 % | 71.6 % | **7.7 ± 1.3 %** (4.7) | −42.09 |

- Money is now negative for everyone: the site must buy ~25 points of charge per car; the question is how much it pays for it. AFAP-target pays the most (−47.9 €/day), the perfect planner the least (−23.6).
- **RiskAware more than halves the share of early leavers driving off below 70 %** (λ 500: 25.7 % → 11.8 %; λ 1000: 20.6 % → 7.7 %), for about 1.8–3.8 € per day.
- **The money-vs-safety trade-off holds again:** RiskAware at λ 500 pays less than Trusting at λ 1000 (−35.26 vs −38.33 €/day) *and* leaves fewer early leavers below 70 % (11.8 % vs 20.6 %). Training with early departures does more than raising the penalty. *Not yet a formal test* (Phase 5).
- Optimum-announced protects early leavers no better than PPO-Trusting (21.7 % below 70 %): perfect planning without knowing about early departures does not help them.

### 3. How RiskAware hedges (`hedging_p0.2.png`)
Same mechanism as in the light case, but stronger:
- RiskAware **sells much less at the start of the stay** (λ 500: ~0.76 vs ~1.25 kWh per car and step in the first 10 %) and **fills the car earlier**: it reaches the target at about 55 % of the announced stay at λ 500 (λ 1000: ~45 %); Trusting never gets above target at λ 500 and reaches it only at ~65 % at λ 1000.
- In the window where early leavers drive off (50–90 % of the announced stay) RiskAware keeps a buffer of about **+4 points** above target at λ 500 and **+7.5 points** at λ 1000; Trusting stays at or just below target.
- RiskAware sells a little more at the end of the stay, once the risky window is over.
- PPO-Oracle again keeps **no** buffer and sells like Trusting at the start: it knows who leaves early, so it needs no insurance. The buffer is a response to hidden information.

## Caveats
- **PPO stays far from the perfect planner:** at 0 % early (λ 500) PPO-Trusting scores −43.3 vs −24.0 for Optimum-real, a gap of 19 points, the same as in the light case (−7.3 vs +12.1); here it beats AFAP-target (−48.2) by only 5 points. All agents share the same training budget (6M steps), so the comparison between them is fair, but absolute PPO numbers leave room. RiskAware λ 1000 was still improving at the end of training (best checkpoint in the last 20 % of training for 9 of 10 seeds; validation +1.5 in the last fifth), so its benefit may be underestimated.
- Only RiskAware p0.2 was re-run (no p0.05 / p0.1, no λ 200) and the Oracle only at λ 500.
- One 80 % target for every driver; synthetic early-departure rule (ACN-Data planned in Phase 6).
- Scores at different λ use different rewards and can only be compared through money and battery levels.
- 12 runs (λ 500 RiskAware s0–s9, Oracle s3–s4) record `git dirty = true` at commit 80e61d6: the only uncommitted change was `max_parallel` in the sweep files (committed in 254e8e2), which does not affect results.
