# Phase 4 main grid: does training with hidden early departures help?

**Runs:** 140, all finished, none failed (2026-11-22, sweeps `p4-main-grid` and `p4-oracle`).
**Setup:** frozen ADR-0007 (`configs/experiments/p4_main.yaml`): one PPO for the site, imitation warm start, 6M steps, best model picked on 30 validation days. Every agent is tested on the same 50 test days in 4 worlds (0 / 5 / 10 / 20 % of drivers leave early without warning, cutting their stay by 10–50 %).

| Agent | Trained in | Sees real departures? | λ_dep | Seeds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PPO-Trusting | 0 % early | no | 200 / 500 / 1000 | 0–9 |
| PPO-RiskAware p0.05 / p0.1 / p0.2 (ours) | 5 / 10 / 20 % early | no | 200 / 500 / 1000 | 0–9 |
| PPO-Oracle p0.1 / p0.2 | 10 / 20 % early | **yes** | 500 | 0–9 |
| AFAP-target, Optimum-announced, Optimum-real | — (rules / perfect planner) | — / no / yes | each λ | — |

Reward per day (ADR-0006): R = R_econ − λ_over · max(0, P_tot − P_max)² − λ_dep · Σ max(0, 0.8 − SoC)²
(money earned − grid overload penalty − penalty for every car leaving below 80 %, squared).

## Files (generated by `scripts/make_figures.py`, never by hand)
One folder per λ: `lam200/`, `lam500/` (with PPO-Oracle), `lam1000/`.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `summary.csv` | per agent and test world: score, money, penalties, % drivers at target, … (mean, std, 95 % CI over 10 seeds) |
| `paired.csv` | every agent − PPO-Trusting on the same seed: difference, 95 % CI, wins out of 10, t-test p, Holm-corrected p |
| `drivers.csv` | battery level when cars leave (all cars, early leavers, worst 10 % of early leavers, % of early leavers below 70 %), incl. AFAP-target and optimum |
| `grid.png` | train/test grid: score in every test world |
| `hedging_p*.png` | battery level above target and energy sold over the announced stay |
| `learning.png` | validation score during training |

Command (λ 500; λ 200 / 1000 the same without the two Oracle groups):
```bash
G() { echo "$1=results/*_p4-main-lam500-$2_s?"; }
python scripts/make_figures.py --group "$(G PPO-Trusting trusting)" \
  --group "$(G 'RiskAware p0.05' riskaware-p0.05)" --group "$(G 'RiskAware p0.1' riskaware-p0.1)" \
  --group "$(G 'RiskAware p0.2' riskaware-p0.2)" --group "$(G 'Oracle p0.1' oracle-p0.1)" \
  --group "$(G 'Oracle p0.2' oracle-p0.2)" --optimum "results/*_optimum-test-lamdep500" \
  --out reports/p4-main-grid/lam500
```

## Results

### 1. Score: RiskAware p0.2 − Trusting, same seed (`paired.csv`)
Positive = RiskAware better. p (Holm) is corrected over all comparisons in that λ folder.

| λ | Test world | Difference | 95 % CI | Wins / 10 | p | p (Holm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | 0 % early | −2.19 | ±1.84 | 1 | 0.025 | 0.30 |
| 200 | 20 % early | +0.68 | ±1.57 | 6 | 0.35 | 1.00 |
| 500 | 0 % early | −1.38 | ±1.68 | 3 | 0.095 | 0.95 |
| 500 | 10 % early | +2.44 | ±1.56 | 9 | 0.006 | 0.094 |
| 500 | 20 % early | **+5.52** | ±1.41 | **10** | <0.001 | **<0.001** |
| 1000 | 0 % early | −2.60 | ±1.82 | 1 | 0.010 | 0.11 |
| 1000 | 20 % early | **+8.75** | ±3.31 | **10** | <0.001 | **0.002** |

- The gain grows with the penalty λ and with the share of early leavers; it also grows with the training risk (p0.05 < p0.1 < p0.2 at λ 500 and 1000, see `paired.csv`).
- When nobody leaves early, RiskAware pays an "insurance" of about 1.4–2.6 points per day (not significant after correction).
- At λ 200 undercharging is cheap, and training with early departures gives no benefit.
- PPO-Oracle (λ 500) scores −7.4 / −7.2 at 0 / 20 % early (flat): at 20 % early, RiskAware p0.2 (−11.4) closes about 60 % of the gap between Trusting (−16.9) and the Oracle.

### 2. Battery level when cars leave, 20 % early world (`drivers.csv`, `summary.csv`)
Mean over 10 seeds (95 % CI for the share below 70 %). Cars arrive with 74.7 % on average.

| λ | Agent | All cars | Early leavers | Worst 10 % of early leavers | Early leavers below 70 % | Money per day (€) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| any | AFAP-target | 83.0 % | 83.5 % | 80.4 % | 0 % | −15.07 |
| 500 | Optimum-real | 78.7 % | 79.0 % | 77.3 % | 0 % | +12.38 |
| 200 | PPO-Trusting | 77.1 % | 78.8 % | 64.4 % | 23.1 ± 1.8 % | +9.46 |
| 200 | RiskAware p0.2 | 77.0 % | 78.7 % | 66.5 % | 17.5 ± 2.1 % | +8.50 |
| 500 | PPO-Trusting | 79.9 % | 79.3 % | 65.3 % | 18.9 ± 2.4 % | +1.96 |
| 500 | RiskAware p0.2 | 80.8 % | 82.1 % | 70.4 % | 9.5 ± 1.8 % | +0.45 |
| 500 | PPO-Oracle p0.1 | 80.5 % | 81.3 % | 74.5 % | 1.9 ± 0.5 % | +0.08 |
| 1000 | PPO-Trusting | 82.5 % | 81.7 % | 68.2 % | 13.7 ± 2.5 % | −2.84 |
| 1000 | RiskAware p0.2 | 84.5 % | 86.6 % | 73.3 % | 4.7 ± 1.4 % | −5.25 |

- **A better money-vs-safety trade-off:** RiskAware p0.2 at λ 500 earns more than PPO-Trusting at λ 1000 (+0.45 vs −2.84 €/day) and leaves fewer early leavers below 70 % (9.5 % vs 13.7 %). Training with early departures does more than raising the penalty. *Not yet a formal test* (Phase 5: money-vs-safety curve with confidence intervals).
- RiskAware protects the **tail**: average levels are close, but the share of early leavers driving off below 70 % halves at λ 500 and drops by two thirds at λ 1000.
- Even the perfect planner stops just below 80 % (Optimum-real: average shortfall 1.4 points), because the squared penalty makes small misses nearly free. A low "% of drivers exactly at target" is therefore a property of the reward, not a learning failure.

### 3. How RiskAware hedges (`hedging_p0.2.png`)
At λ 500 and 1000, RiskAware **fills each car earlier** and keeps a larger battery buffer above target in the window where early leavers drive off (50–90 % of the announced stay): about +3–4 points at λ 500 and +5–8 points at λ 1000, vs about 0–3 for Trusting. The buffer grows with the training risk (clearly ordered p0.05 < p0.1 < p0.2 at λ 1000; p0.1 ≈ p0.2 at λ 500). RiskAware sells a little less at the start of the stay and a little more at the end, once the risky window is over.

PPO-Oracle keeps **no** buffer (same curve as Trusting): it knows who will leave early, so it needs no insurance and still protects drivers best. This shows the buffer really is a response to hidden information, not a generally better charging habit.

## Caveats
- **Cars arrive almost charged** (74.7 % on average vs the 80 % target; 37 % of cars arrive above target). EV2Gym sets the arrival level as "100 % − the energy the driver takes (ElaadNL data)", but our target is 80 % (ADR-0006), so drivers need only about 5 points on average. Planned fix: ADR-0008 (arrival = 80 % − the energy from the data), followed by a focused re-run. These results then become the "light charging need" case.
- One 80 % target for every driver (per-driver targets from ACN-Data in Phase 6).
- Early departures follow a synthetic rule (ACN-Data in Phase 6).
- Scores at different λ use different rewards and can only be compared through money and battery levels, not through the score.
- The first 11 main-grid jobs record `git dirty = true` (Oracle code was being added; backward-compatible, see EXPERIMENT_LOG).
